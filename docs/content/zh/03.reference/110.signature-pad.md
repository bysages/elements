## SignaturePad

The one-tag path: the paper, guide, clear control, and hidden form
value; stroke previews and drawing events stay on the anatomy.

## Root

| 属性 | 类型 | 默认值 | 描述 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `defaultPaths` | `string[]` |  | 签名板的默认路径 |
