## 属性

### Container

| 属性 | 类型 | 默认值 | 描述 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `size` | `String` | `"readable"` | 内容排版宽度，可为 `narrow`、`readable`、`wide` 或 `full` |
| `padding` | `Boolean` | `true` | — |
