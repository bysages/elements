## 属性

### Tour

—

| 属性 | 类型 | 默认值 | 描述 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tour` | `Object as PropType<UseTourReturn["value"]>` | — | — |
| `trigger` | `String` | `"Start tour"` | — |
| `portal` | `[Boolean, String] as PropType<FloatingPortalTarget>` | `true` | 水合后将浮层渲染到 `body`（`true`）或指定选择器；`false` 保持原位渲染。 |
