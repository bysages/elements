## 属性

### Calendar

Calendar：日历网格，即日期选择器的月份视图，不带弹层。它始终展开，没有触发器，容器呈现为一张低调卡片。标题可在月份和年份网格之间切换层级；网格复用日期选择器的状态机，包括取值、范围选择和焦点。

| 属性 | 类型 | 默认值 | 描述 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `modelValue` | `null as unknown as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["modelValue"]>` | `undefined` | 选中的日期；日期范围以数组表示，并由状态机处理 |
| `defaultValue` | `null as unknown as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["defaultValue"]>` | `undefined` | — |
| `min` | `null as unknown as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["min"]>` | `undefined` | — |
| `max` | `null as unknown as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["max"]>` | `undefined` | — |
| `variant` | `String as PropType<"compact" \| "full">` | `"compact"` | compact keeps the popup's measure; full gives cells room for content. |
| `mode` | `String as PropType<"month" \| "year">` | `"month"` | month is the day grid; year starts on a month overview. |
| `selectionMode` | `String as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["selectionMode"]>` | `undefined` | — |
| `startOfWeek` | `Number` | `undefined` | — |
| `fixedWeeks` | `Boolean` | `false` | — |
| `showWeekNumbers` | `Boolean` | `false` | — |
| `outsideDaySelectable` | `Boolean` | `true` | — |
| `closeOnSelect` | `Boolean` | `true` | — |
| `minView` | `String as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["minView"]>` | `undefined` | — |
| `maxView` | `String as PropType<DatePickerRootProps["maxView"]>` | `undefined` | — |
