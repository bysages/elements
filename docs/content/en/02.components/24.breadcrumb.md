## Props

### BreadcrumbRoot

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### BreadcrumbList

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### BreadcrumbItem

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### BreadcrumbLink

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### BreadcrumbCurrent

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### BreadcrumbSeparator

A styled part — no props of its own; it takes the anatomy's shared styling.

### SBreadcrumb

The one-tag trail: plain strings or named waymarks, with the current final waymark rendered as the current page.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `items` | `Array as PropType<Array<string \| BreadcrumbItem>>` | — | The trail, read in order; the last waymark is the current page. |
| `label` | `String` | `undefined` | The trail's accessible name, in place of the shared default. |
