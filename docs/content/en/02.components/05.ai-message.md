## Props

### AiMessage

Whose stroke this is — the user's words sit in a recessed bubble, the assistant speaks flat on the paper. The generic `MESSAGE extends AiSdkMessage` follows the application's AI SDK `UIMessage` contract.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `role` | `String as PropType<"user" \| "assistant" \| "system">` | `"assistant"` | Whose stroke this is — the user's words sit in a recessed bubble, the assistant speaks flat on the paper. |
| `message` | `Object as PropType<UIMessage>` | `undefined` | The AI SDK message; its id and role become the article identity. |
